Yell for business

Lead UX / UI designer - iOS, Android & Responsive web

// Project aim

Yell wanted to release a series of new features to enhance their offering to make finding a professional as frictionless as possible.


So we looked a various different options for the 'Find a quote' idea and produced a prototype to gauge where their users were at and how we could serve this business need to the user.


Tools used; Figma (UI & prototype), MIRO, Usability hub, Optimal sort.
